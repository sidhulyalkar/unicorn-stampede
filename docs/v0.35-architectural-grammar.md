# v0.35 research: architectural grammar

## Research question

Can Unicorn Stampede replace enough of the hand-written generic-building renderer with a procedural architectural grammar that is **smaller after the actual Terser → Roadroller → ZIP pipeline while producing more distinct buildings**?

Yes, at the compression and structural-diversity level. The tuned grammar is now integrated on this research branch. Human hands-on visual review remains the final promotion gate.

## Safe research baseline

The integration is based on gameplay commit `6268869cf80e768da031a9e8e3da340b1e948917`, the latest fully-qualified head before a later parallel cleanup temporarily broke the release-prune contract.

That base passed the complete behavior and packed-browser suite, including landmark-driven herd progression, and packed to **13,164 / 13,312 bytes** with **148 bytes free**.

A later moving gameplay head was deliberately not used because its game tests passed but `scripts/build.mjs` failed with `release prune failed`. Renderer research should not be promoted on top of a known-red packaging base.

## What was replaced

Only the generic Bakery / Bank / Books / Cafe / Florist facade body was changed.

The following remain untouched:

- fountain
- pond
- hedges
- market stall renderer
- clock tower renderer
- destruction rubble
- damage tint
- building nameplates
- HP, value, collision, scoring and district behavior

No new RNG state is added. Architectural variation is derived deterministically from existing building geometry.

## Control

The old generic facade renderer used five bespoke branches and occupied **1,164 source bytes**. Its visual vocabulary was hand-authored per family.

## Experiment 1: a generic feature-mask decoder lost

The first attempt generalized those branches into a small feature-mask interpreter. It was shorter in source but worse after Roadroller:

| Candidate | Facade source delta | Reachable states | Paired ZIP delta |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `grammar-compact` | -228 B | 44 | **+45 B** |
| `grammar-depth` | -127 B | 88 | **+60 B** |

This established the central compression lesson: **source length is not the objective. Roadroller-visible entropy is.** The repetitive old branches compress surprisingly well; a generic decoder can be shorter yet cost more in the final archive.

## Experiment 2: repeated architectural language won

The successful direction is a small repeated visual language whose parameters create variation.

A lower-bound `grammar-rhythm` proved that a shared bay / row / cornice vocabulary could expose far more states and materially shrink the cartridge.

## Semantic cue ablation

We then priced family identity one cue at a time after Roadroller:

| Cue | Marginal packed cost |
| --- | ---: |
| Bank roof / pediment | **+10 B** |
| Bakery awning | **+55 B** |
| Books masonry | **+2 B** |
| Florist flowers | **+19 B** |

The result is useful beyond this renderer: not all visible detail has equal compressed cost. Books masonry is nearly free; the awning is comparatively expensive.

## Identity grammar

The final architecture encodes identity in two layers.

### Shared procedural structure

Each generic building derives:

- hue-derived shell material
- 3–6 bay rhythm
- one or two opening rows
- deterministic geometry seed from position
- family-specific cornice count `2 + family`

The unique cornice count means every family has a distinct structural signature even before special ornament is considered.

### High-information family cues

- Bakery: segmented awning using the existing bay count
- Bank: pediment using the existing bay count
- Books: repeated masonry courses
- Cafe: unique cornice / opening / hue rhythm without an extra special-case ornament
- Florist: flower accents

The five resulting family signatures have **zero structural collisions** and a minimum pairwise structural distance of **2**.

## Tuned integrated candidate

The production candidate further reuses already-computed grammar parameters:

- tighter cornice spacing: `18 + 8i` → `12 + 6i`
- Bank pediment reuses bay count `n`
- Bakery canopy reuses bay count `n`

Final generic-facade source:

- legacy: **1,164 B**
- tuned grammar: **674 B**
- source reduction: **490 B**

## Authoritative paired result

On the safe qualified `6268869c…` packing lineage, both renderers were built through the same production compression matrix.

| Renderer | Facade source | Reachable states | Structural collisions | Min family distance | Final ZIP | Reserve |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| legacy bespoke renderer | 1,164 B | 5 | n/a | n/a | **13,162 B** | 150 B |
| tuned semantic grammar | **674 B** | **120** | **0** | **2** | **13,100 B** | **212 B** |

Paired packed improvement: **-62 bytes**.

This is the desired Pareto result: the renderer exposes a much larger architectural state space while the complete Roadroller + ZIP cartridge becomes smaller.

A stripped identity-only core can reach 13,035 B, but it intentionally leaves more family personality on the floor. The semantic grammar is selected because the extra cues are worth the bytes and still leave substantial reserve.

## Realized town diversity

The 120-state count is grammar capacity, not a claim that one town contains 120 buildings.

Sampling the actual current building-placement logic across campaign towns produced roughly **21–26 generic buildings per town**, with approximately **18–23 distinct facade descriptors realized in a single town**. Most buildings therefore exercise different structural descriptors in practice rather than merely sharing five templates under a theoretical combinatorial ceiling.

## Post-integration contract

`scripts/grammar-research.mjs` is no longer an experiment mutator. It now verifies the integrated renderer directly and fails if:

- the procedural identity cues disappear
- the legacy bespoke facade table returns
- facade source exceeds 700 bytes
- reachable descriptor states fall below 100
- family structural distance falls below 2
- any family signatures collide
- the final ZIP exceeds 13,312 bytes

The historical paired result above is retained as provenance.

## Promotion gates still open

The byte and structural-diversity hypotheses are now supported. Promotion still requires:

1. exact-head full game qualification after integration
2. exact-head grammar contract qualification
3. hands-on visual inspection of Bakery / Bank / Books / Cafe / Florist during real movement
4. confirmation that hue-derived materials remain readable under paint, damage tint, particles and combat clutter
5. confirmation that generated variety feels intentional rather than noisy

The PR should remain draft until those visual gates are explicitly reviewed.

## Result

The research direction is viable. More importantly, the useful formulation is now sharper:

> **Do not optimize architectural JavaScript for source characters. Optimize the repeated visual language for compressed entropy and perceptual information.**

For this renderer, that has produced the strange little jackpot we were looking for: **more distinct architecture, fewer shipped bytes.**
