# v0.62 quality-first submission frontier

This branch exists to search for a legal 13 KB submission from the v0.61 gameplay-quality authority without regressing the town back into the visually compromised RC2 style.

The baseline is the hardened v0.61 product. Experiments must be release-time transformations wherever possible. The development build remains readable and high quality.

The first frontier is measured, not guessed: particles, release hooks, wrapper staticization, compact moving-pedestrian logic, repeated guidance strings, and procedural building-recipe representation. Visual microcuts are deferred until those representation-level experiments are exhausted.
